# Idea Backlog

This file captures possibilities without promising that they will be implemented.

## Status labels

- **Inbox:** Recorded but not evaluated
- **Exploring:** Worth discussing or prototyping
- **Planned:** Approved and moved to the roadmap
- **On hold:** Potentially useful later
- **Rejected:** Intentionally excluded, with the reason preserved
- **Implemented:** Present in the game

## Combat ideas

### Full-card ability candidates

**Status:** Exploring. [Eight candidate rules, tradeoffs and thematic fits](CARD_ABILITY_IDEAS.md) include Slipstream, Formation, Towline, Backwash, Breakwater, Territorial, Relay and Broadside. Prototype the first three before assigning them to the roster. These are not playable abilities yet.

### Weight classes

**Status:** Exploring

Tiny, medium, and heavy fish could require different amounts of force to push.

### Currents

**Status:** Exploring

Board currents could move fish, modify pushes, or change direction as the tide advances.

### Environmental tiles

**Status:** Inbox

Possible tiles include kelp, rocks, deep water, whirlpools, thermal vents, and polluted water.

### Schooling

**Status:** Inbox

Related or adjacent fish could gain positional benefits.

### Predators

**Status:** Inbox

Predatory fish could threaten smaller fish through a mechanic distinct from ordinary pushing.

### Stranding

**Status:** Exploring

Fish pushed off the board could become stranded or knocked out until they are hydrated.

### Tide timer

**Status:** Exploring

Matches could end after a fixed number of rounds, with the tide providing the thematic clock.

## Progression ideas

### Colossal decks and creature rewards

**Status:** Planned for themed decks; Exploring for exclusive cards and rewards. Colossals remain last, after the ordinary roster pass.

Give each Colossal a coherent themed deck that supports its identity and battle plan. It may contain exclusive creatures. Defeating a Colossal could award one of those creatures for the remainder of the current Voyage, giving the victory a playable reward in later regions. Exclusivity, reward choice versus fixed grant, and whether a boss card needs a player-appropriate variant remain undecided. A final-region reward has no remaining normal voyage encounters, so its purpose needs a separate decision; do not assume permanent unlocks or postgame content.
