# Bermuda — complete regional role and balance pass

All **43/43** Bermuda entries are reviewed: **23 layouts changed, 20 retained**. This includes the original Hypno Squid and all 42 supplied cards. Every creature has an individually authored primary identity, ordinary-play plan, limitation and keep/change decision. The [roster inventory](ROSTER_ROLE_INVENTORY.md#bermuda--43-creatures) supplies the full roles and revision-scoped relative component ranges; the journal displays advice only for copies matching current rules.

The three regional passes now cover **167/167 catalog entries**. This completes the role-review checklist, not a promise of perfect equality. New mechanics, prices, rarity, art, pool membership, fishing and full-card abilities are unchanged. Stable texture IDs remain intact even where names changed earlier: Graveyard Fleet uses `graveyard-leviathan`, Drowned Admiral uses `tempest-seahorse`, and Lightning Skeleton uses `stormfin-tuna`. Their roles follow their current names and artwork rather than obsolete IDs. Reef Titan and other large regular cards are not implementations of the postponed Colossal bosses.

## Internal family decisions

| Batch | Anchors, review and decision |
| --- | --- |
| Wreck arrangers | Ghost Net anchors gap-dependent Hook. Crabs/lobsters/fishermen use claws, tethers and shelter rather than incidental line attacks. Anchor/Chain/Figurehead keep ordinary setup with a primary signature; Rust/Squall share a Hook claw with distinct lower shell/storm coverage. Drowned Diver keeps a tether, dive and shell-side protection. |
| Ambushers | Shoreline Moray and Ocean Barracuda remain simpler Bite anchors. Phantom/Void retain earlier tested reductions. Shipwreck Moray has a conditional vault/grasp route. Bonefish loses a second escape mode. Mimic gains delivery/counterplay. Reef Titan becomes a claw-and-jaw guardian. Bottomless Maw and Sharktopus retain deliberate opposed-versus-corner Hook/Bite gimmicks with friendly-kill risk. |
| Wake drivers | Ocean Sunfish anchors one fan, Humpback a broad wave/line package. Cannonball keeps meaningful recoil; Ghost Ship keeps anchor and wake coverage with compulsory collateral. Fleet loses an unrelated Bite; Mountain replaces Bite/vault with a heavy corner wake; Stormback loses Bite/Ram and keeps two fans plus ordinary setup. |
| Storm support | Shoreline Pistol Shrimp anchors shutdown plus setup. Lighthouse gains shell shelter and loses Ram/Spines clutter. Lightning Skeleton becomes a forked-bolt striker rather than an obsolete tuna-shaped pursuer; Admiral becomes a formation/signal setter. Bilge, Buoy, Lightning Marlin and Thunder Jelly retain specific shutdown origins and collateral. Black Squall keeps pursuit and recoil but loses incidental Shock after testing showed that removing recoil hurt its ordinary use. |
| Spatial arrangers | Hypno Squid teaches the defended-target limitation of Swap. Compass, Triangle and Map lose incidental coverage but keep specific exchange routes. Living Whirlpool and Bottle Ocean keep different exchange/fan axes and orders. Escher's staircase and Eye's moving shutdown center remain deliberate sequences. Red Door keeps its coherent four-Swap gimmick; a test shows the complete chain can undo its first gain. |
| Escape movers | Ocean divers remain simpler movement anchors. Spadefish loses incidental Shock, Fossil loses Shock, and Paradox becomes a spined escape card rather than a Bite/Shock cocktail. Mobius and Split-Timeline retain intentional multi-stage movement puzzles; their edges never repeat and blocked stages alter the final location. |

Four occupied edges are not automatically forbidden or justified by rarity. Retained four-edge cards have explicit repeated vocabulary or a specific sequence; broad reach carries mandatory allied displacement, conditional origins, or delivery costs. Removed flanks are tested with ordinary Standard replies. Shielded cards must have a delivery path as well as a counter.

## Tests and interpretation

```powershell
node --test tests/bermuda-region.test.mjs tests/bermuda-cards.test.mjs

```

The role probe enumerates every legal placement across 12 contexts × 3 sizes × 2 orientations (72 fixtures per version per creature). Six internal family tests require ordinary 7×7 reef control or shutdown. Extra tests cover claws against Shields, friendly delivery, Mimic's old and new delivery route, safe replies through 18 newly open flanks, Bite collateral, allied Shock, Red Door's complete chain, and saved/recruited/rival definitions and seeded offers across all supported balance versions.

Mixed-school comparisons change only the candidate on the experimental side; every other card and the rival use frozen balance version 5. Two helper schools mix ordinary Shoreline/Ocean cards with Bermuda tools. Each condition is two reset tides with swapped starters, a 7×7 board, six plays per side, Expert policies, regional terrain, and either no charms or one of four inventories covering all 12 charms. Charms are player-only. Whole-pool groups compare the entire old and revised catalog on both sides.

Final evidence: [seeds 0–3](BERMUDA_EVIDENCE_0.md) and [seeds 4–7](BERMUDA_EVIDENCE_4.md). **3,440 paired comparisons / 13,760 tides**, plus **96 whole-pool two-tide games / 192 tides**, total **13,952 final-evidence tides**. All **43/43** revised entries actually played in both whole-pool batches, and every individually compared candidate was selected. Six wrapped eight-card groups cover the entire pool.

The first proposal removed Black Squall's recoil and made Lightning Skeleton's right edge Standard. Their first-batch margins fell from 0.525 to −0.425 and 0.6 to −0.1. A focused 160-tide check on fresh seeds 8/9 tested retaining recoil while removing Shock, and a Double right edge for Skeleton: 0.7 → 0.85 and 0.9 → 1.15 respectively. The final design adopts those refinements. Their original seed comparisons and every whole-pool game were rerun; the other 41 isolated comparisons retained exactly the same candidate and frozen helper/rival definitions. Lightning Skeleton's final original-seed margins (−0.05 and 0.125) remain below its old overstuffed package; the fresh-seed reversal is evidence of context sensitivity, not proof of a buff. It now has two shutdown angles and a penetrating setup strike, ordinary Shield/Double counters, an exposed underside and friendly-Shock risk. This is a deliberate narrower role, anchored against Pistol Shrimp and Electric Eel, not a claim of equal match power. Drowned Diver also loses 0.6 in the second batch after losing Ram; its useful vault/tether/shelter role remains verified. Triangle Shard changes sign between batches. These are priorities for human school-building feedback, not grounds to restore arbitrary edges from a single mean.

Whole-pool player W/D/L: seeds 0–3 **12/4/8 → 15/6/3**; seeds 4–7 **12/3/9 → 17/1/6**. Mean distinct casualties across both tides: **3.29 → 4.29**, and **3.88 → 4.17**. The more open flanks increase removal opportunities even though several incidental Bites were removed. These asymmetric, charm-aided samples do not establish a fair win rate or settle long-Voyage recovery pressure. Their casualty increase is recorded for subsequent route/playtesting rather than silently counted as an improvement.


Eight shared seed identifiers and repeated templates are not thousands of independent observations. Policy results are bounded Expert search, not human choices or a learned metagame. The match helper keeps its historical public-threat sample fixed across comparisons; this does not measure adaptation of hidden-hand predictions to the revised catalog. Actual played cards resolve their selected catalog's mechanics. Player-only charms and asymmetric schools preclude calling player W/D/L a fair-seat balance rate. Two-tide casualty counts do not model complete-Voyage attrition. No full human match or pacing study was performed. Relative weights remain revisable; no fixed sum decided whether a card passed.

## Progression, saves and verification

Shoreline and Open Ocean are unchanged. Bermuda retains Swap as its new regional vocabulary and combines established Hook, Shock, movement and wake mechanics. Familiar claws, shells and predators remain recognizable; the spatial curios retain deliberate complexity. Existing pool/zone acquisition behavior is unchanged, so this is not a guaranteed teaching sequence.

New voyages use balance version **6**. Version **5** is frozen in `BATCH_FIVE_STARTERS`; prior historical catalogs derive through it. Existing voyages retain schools, future recruits, rival definitions and seeded offers. Quick Match uses current layouts. Journal advice is hidden for older copies whose mechanics differ.

Full verification passed **245 tests**, workflow configuration, type checking and the production build (existing bundle-size warning). Browser verification uses an isolated in-memory save fixture with all 43 school entries and the changed native-scale card renderers; current Mimic shows its Standard delivery edge and role/tradeoff, while a saved three-Shield copy keeps its old edges without incompatible advice. Native layouts and larger storm/spatial views were inspected again after the two refinements.

The regional roster review is complete. Next useful work is player testing of mixed schools and starting-school choices using these roles; Colossal bosses and themed boss decks remain last.
