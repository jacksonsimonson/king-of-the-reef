# Carried-school rival strength experiment

Follow-up to [the full Voyage integration pass](VOYAGE_INTEGRATION.md). Test small ordinary-rival target-rating reductions against identical carried Open Ocean and Bermuda schools. **Reject both candidates for live gameplay.** Lower heuristic ratings did not reliably produce easier encounters; the initially promising smaller reduction regressed on the larger fresh-seed check.

No creature, rival, AI, reward, elite, boss or save-version values change. Rival playstyle names remain internal.

## Protocol and reproduction

Base: PR #49, `285c37327c0d3dffc403220710bd66764f3cb36b`. Current card balance 7 and weighted rival version 1. Use the pinned Node version.

```powershell
node scripts/balance/carried-rival-study.mjs 0 8
node scripts/balance/carried-rival-evidence.mjs .cache/balance/carried-rivals-0.json
node scripts/balance/carried-rival-study.mjs 8 8
node scripts/balance/carried-rival-evidence.mjs .cache/balance/carried-rivals-8.json
node scripts/balance/carried-rival-study.mjs 16 16
node scripts/balance/carried-rival-evidence.mjs .cache/balance/carried-rivals-16.json
node --test tests/rival-prototype.test.mjs
npm.cmd run verify
```

Generate 64 baseline Voyages on 32 seeds (`INTEGRATION-v7-0` through `31`), with baseline or deliberate recruiting/trimming, recovery routes, elite avoidance, 85% assumed fishing success and Expert player decisions throughout. Capture the first two ordinary encounters reached in each later region per run. Remove duplicate snapshots where seed, node, school and charms match. Failed early runs contribute no later encounters; this is a survivor-selected sample, not an estimate of universal encounter difficulty.

The result is **104 distinct carried encounters: 80 Open Ocean and 24 Bermuda**. Replay each under the current target and reductions of 0.1 and 0.2 regional card-rating standard deviations: **312 combined matches / 624 tides**, additional to the baseline Voyages used to collect schools. Raw fixtures and results remain in ignored `.cache/balance/` and are regenerated by the commands above.

The study-only sampler keeps the same 16 candidate schools, full regional pool, no-replacement draw, style weights, positioning minimum and random seed. Only the target used to select among those candidates changes. Tests check zero-offset parity against production across all five styles and both regions; each captured encounter also checks exact parity against `rivalDeckFor`. The prototype is never imported by the game.

Paired replays preserve the carried player cards and injuries, starting charms, deal/terrain seed, regional AI, 5/6 plays per tide and both starting orders. Charm decisions follow the same deterministic policy, but may differ when the board changes; we do not force an illegal or irrelevant identical action. Each replay must leave the stored run untouched. Candidates do not feed back into later recruitment or routes, deliberately isolating encounter effects.

Seeds 0–7 are discovery; 8–15 are the first fresh-seed check. After the smaller reduction showed limited benefit, seeds 16–31 were added as a larger confirmation check. Both candidates were recorded throughout; no search over additional settings followed their outcomes. Matches sharing a map seed or carried school are correlated; these are descriptive comparisons, not significance claims.

## Results

W/D/L refers to the player's combined two-tide outcome. A positive margin change means a better player score margin than the paired current setting.

| Batch | Region | Current W/D/L | −0.1 SD W/D/L | −0.1 margin change | −0.2 SD W/D/L | −0.2 margin change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Discovery 0–7 | Ocean | 1 / 1 / 14 | 1 / 5 / 10 | +0.500 | 0 / 4 / 12 | −0.250 |
| Discovery 0–7 | Bermuda | 0 / 0 / 4 | 0 / 0 / 4 | +0.750 | 0 / 0 / 4 | 0.000 |
| Fresh 8–15 | Ocean | 0 / 3 / 13 | 1 / 2 / 13 | +0.188 | 0 / 2 / 14 | −0.188 |
| Fresh 8–15 | Bermuda | 0 / 0 / 6 | 0 / 1 / 5 | +0.333 | 0 / 0 / 6 | −0.833 |
| Confirmation 16–31 | Ocean | 4 / 9 / 35 | 1 / 10 / 37 | −0.063 | 4 / 7 / 37 | −0.125 |
| Confirmation 16–31 | Bermuda | 0 / 0 / 14 | 0 / 1 / 13 | +0.500 | 0 / 2 / 12 | +0.786 |

The larger confirmation batch spans 14 Ocean map seeds and five Bermuda map seeds. The smaller reduction improves eight Ocean encounter outcomes but worsens eleven. For Bermuda, its average improvement is not consistent across seeds: three of five seed-average margins worsen, while two improve. The larger reduction also reverses its Bermuda trend between batches. Neither supports a dependable general difficulty reduction.

The sampler does lower its numerical rating as intended. In confirmation, mean Ocean ratings move from 3.622 to 3.569 / 3.512, and Bermuda from 4.246 to 4.188 / 4.141. That is a change in a provisional composition heuristic, not a measured percentage decrease in battle strength. Card interactions and directional coverage can outweigh a small rating difference.

## Decision and next useful step

Keep current weights and save compatibility. Do not continue lowering the target on the assumption that a smaller number must mean a weaker school. Keep the heuristic for approximate composition budgets until it has demonstrated predictive value for battle outcomes.

The next useful diagnostic is a controlled comparison separating **rival school composition** from **rival decision strength**: replay these same snapshots with current schools and adjacent AI difficulty settings, then compare a small set of human-played carried encounters. This can identify whether to adjust decision strength, recruitment access/composition, or particular rival combinations. It is a proposed diagnostic, not approval to flatten the three regional difficulties or rewrite the roster. Avoid another broad card rebalance without attribution.

Bespoke Colossals remain last, and starting-school selection remains shelved. The later-region difficulty concern is still unresolved; rejecting these prototypes is not balance sign-off.

Validation: all 295 tests, workflow checks, type-check and build pass; the existing large-bundle warning remains. No production or visual changes, and no new browser playtest. These simulations do not establish human pacing, fishing skill, charm mastery, or whether losing encounters feel fair.
