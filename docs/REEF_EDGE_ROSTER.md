# Reef Edge Roster

Implemented roster: 76 Reef cards, 46 newly playable. No new full-card effects. Rally and Revelation retain their existing behavior. Standard arrows have 15 distinct nonempty layouts, assigned exclusively to ordinary fish. Edge identity ignores full-card abilities and compares all four directions.

Only Standard, Double, Shield, Hook, Shock, Spines, and Bite appear in the Reef. Wave and Swap remain outside this pool. Non-gimmick cards have at most two special edges and at least one blank side. Exceptions: Boxfish (four Shields), Electric Eel (four Shock edges), Sea Urchin (four Spines), and Giant Clam (three Shields and one blank side).

New Voyage rarity weights remain 47% Common, 37% Uncommon, 15% Rare, 1% Extremely Rare. Extremely Rare is shared by Moray Eel and Blacktip Reef Shark. These are initial balance assignments for playtesting, not claims of equal power.

Older Voyages retain their previous draw tables and original creature definitions for new recruits and rivals. Owned cards and in-progress fishing retain their saved state. The Gallery and new Quick Matches use the new designs.

A = Standard Arrow; D = Double; S = Shield; H = Hook; E = Shock; P = Spines; B = Bite; — = blank. Directions refer to player cards.

Biology informs the theme rather than simulating anatomy: the Electric Eel remains a deliberate fantasy Reef resident, Shock represents the Pistol Shrimp’s concussive claw, and passive filter feeders use abstracted defense. Mantis Shrimp striking clubs informed Double ([Monterey Bay Aquarium](https://www.montereybayaquarium.org/animals-the-ocean/animals-a-to-z/peacock-mantis-shrimp)). Tangs intentionally stay basic despite their defensive tail anatomy ([Florida Museum](https://www.floridamuseum.ufl.edu/discover-fish/species-profiles/doctorfish/)).
