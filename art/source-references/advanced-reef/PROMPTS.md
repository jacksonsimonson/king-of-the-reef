# Advanced Reef Sprite Prompts

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, one call per creature. Originals are in this directory. Game-ready sprites are at `public/assets/fish/<id>.png`, prepared using `scripts/prepare_generated_sprite.py` with cluster size 22 and explicit eye corrections. Runtime rendering is exactly 64px / 128px.

## parrotfish

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: single game sprite for King of the Reef. Subject: a turquoise and coral-pink parrotfish with chunky beak mouth, oval body, yellow fins, tail left and head right. Chunky 16-bit pixel art: dark outline, readable silhouette, 7 opaque colors plus PURE WHITE eye highlights and actual transparent background. Draw as a logical 64x64 pixel sprite enlarged with perfectly uniform square blocks. Entire creature fits within 56x56 logical pixels centered with padding. Eyes must have pure-white highlights distinct from mouth, at least one full logical pixel. Crisp square pixels, simple connected clusters. No antialiasing, smooth gradients, dithering, text, frames, arrows, scenery, shadows or checkerboard.
